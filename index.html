About The Program

What Is Eagle Convergence?

EAGLE CONVERGENCE is the annual convention of the ARISE CONFERENCE — the flagship monthly first-Saturday program of SPAC Church. Now in its 3rd year, Eagle Convergence has become a prophetic gathering point for young people across the nation.

It is a spirit-filled atmosphere where youth discover their path in God, their purpose in life, and their place in destiny. Attendees are exposed to the real-world demands of career, business, and leadership, while being immersed in deep spiritual capacity building.

Whether you are a student, a graduate, an entrepreneur, or simply seeking direction — Eagle Convergence is designed to equip, empower, and elevate you into the fullness of God's plan for your life.
